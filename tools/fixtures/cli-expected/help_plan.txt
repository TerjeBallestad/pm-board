Usage: pm plan <PLAN-id> [--json]
       pm plan create < plan.json
       pm plan list [--sprint ID]

Shows a plan: its project and notes in short, one line per task with its status and open
blockers, and the next task. pm get TASK-NNN reads one task in full; --json gives the whole
plan. pm patch PLAN-NNN changes its title, stage or tasks.
With create: a new plan from JSON on stdin:
{"title": ..., "sprintId": ..., "context": {...}, "tasks": [{"title": ..., "blockedBy": ["Task 1"]}]}

Flags:
  --json                    The whole plan, every task in full

Examples:
  pm plan PLAN-003
  pm get TASK-021
  pm plan create < plan.json
  pm patch PLAN-003 --stage done
--- stderr
